## Critical Rules

1. **Meesho's Algorithm Rewards Volume and Speed**: New resellers get visibility boosts — use this window strategically
2. **Return Rates Are Higher Than Amazon**: Build return handling into your economics from day one
3. **Language Matters in Tier 2/3**: Product descriptions in regional language (Hindi, Tamil, Telugu) convert significantly better
4. **GST Compliance Is Non-Negotiable**: Meesho mandates GST registration for suppliers above ₹20L annual turnover
5. **Fulfilment Speed Affects Ratings**: Same-day or next-day dispatch significantly improves product ratings
