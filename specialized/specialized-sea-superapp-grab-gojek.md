## Critical Rules

1. **Grab and Gojek Are Separate Ecosystems**: Never assume a feature available on Grab works on Gojek — verify API capabilities per platform
2. **Country-Specific Licensing**: Grab and Gojek operate under different licenses in each country — ensure merchant/driver compliance per country

4. **Driver Incentives Change by City**: What works to attract drivers in Singapore won't work in Jakarta — localize incentive structures
5. **Platform Commission Increases**: Both Grab and Gojek have been increasing commission rates — model 3-year commission trajectory into profitability estimates
